Privacy notice

Last updated 15 November 2026

The Ummah Brief respects your privacy. This notice explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

Who we are

The Ummah Brief publishes educational content and digital products at ummahbrief.com. You can reach us at salaam@ummahbrief.com.

What we collect

  • Newsletter, free guide and waiting lists: your email address, and your first name if you give it.
  • Purchases: your name, email address and order details. Payment is handled by PayPal. We never see or store your card details.
  • Mediation requests: your name, email address and what you choose to tell us about the dispute.
  • The Riba Check: the debt figures you type are worked out inside your own browser. They are never sent to us or stored. Only your email address is sent, if you choose to enter it.
  • The Ledger: the story you submit and your email address. Stories are only ever published with identifying details removed.
  • Messages: whatever you send us by email.

How we use it

  • To send what you asked for: the guide, the newsletter, your purchase.
  • To reply to your mediation request or message.
  • To tell you about our own products. Every email has an unsubscribe link.

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with other organisations for their marketing.

Mediation requests

What you share in a mediation request is treated as confidential. It is read only for the purpose of responding to you, it is never used for marketing, and it is never published.

Services we use

We use trusted providers to run the site and deliver email, and they process your information on our behalf: Formspree (forms), Beehiiv and Systeme.io (email and checkout), PayPal (payments), and GitHub Pages and Netlify (hosting). Some of them store data outside your country.

How long we keep it

We keep your email address until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. We keep purchase records for as long as tax law requires. We delete mediation requests once the matter is closed, unless you ask us to keep them.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, and you can unsubscribe at any time. Email salaam@ummahbrief.com and we will respond within 30 days. Depending on where you live, you also have the right to complain to your data protection regulator.

Changes

If this notice changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
