Questions.

I already have The Rizq Code or a Brief.

Then you may not need the pack. Brief buyers can get The Rizq Code for $27 and their first Circle month free, from the email that came with the Brief.

Do I have to stay in the Circle?

No. The first month is free and optional. If you start it and do not want to continue, cancel before the second month and you pay nothing more.

Will it tell me exactly how much zakat I owe?

It walks you through the questions and the sums. Where scholars differ, such as on pensions, shares or the nisab, it tells you to ask a scholar you trust. It is general education, not a fatwa.

Is there a printed planner?

The planner in the pack is a PDF you can print or fill in on screen. A printed edition is sold on Amazon through The Ummah Brief Library.
