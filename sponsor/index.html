Sponsor the Friday email.

Every Friday, Muslim households open one short email from us: a reflection, a real money story and one link. Each issue carries at most one sponsor, clearly labelled, and only one we would be comfortable recommending to our own family.

Who reads it.

Practising Muslims, mostly working adults with families, who want their money to be halal and in order. They found us through our free tools, our Briefs and The Ledger. We share current subscriber numbers, open rates and clicks with every serious enquiry.

Sponsorship opens once the list passes 2,500 readers. Register your interest now and we will contact you then.

What we say yes to.

  • Products and services a Muslim household can use with a clear conscience: books, courses, tools, food, clothing, travel, education.
  • Muslim-owned businesses, especially small ones.
  • Islamic will-writing, charities with transparent accounts, and halal services with independent shariah certification.

What we always say no to.

  • Anything built on riba: loans, credit cards, buy now pay later, interest-bearing accounts.
  • Speculation and gambling, including crypto trading schemes and "get rich" offers.
  • Multi-level marketing and income promises.
  • Anything we would not recommend to our own family.

Financial products are considered only with independent shariah certification and the right regulatory permissions, and may still be declined.

The two placements.

The main sponsor

One per issue. A short paragraph in our voice, a link, and the label "Sponsor". Priced per issue, based on the current list size.

The classified

Up to two short lines near the end of the email, for small Muslim businesses. A lower price, the same standards.

Rates start from about $25 for every 1,000 subscribers, per issue. We never share readers' details with sponsors, and a sponsor never buys our opinion.
