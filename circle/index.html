Questions.

Can I cancel?

Yes, at any time, from your PayPal account or by emailing salaam@ummahbrief.com. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for.

What happens to the Briefs if I leave?

Briefs are part of the membership, so access ends when it ends. If you joined yearly, The Rizq Code is yours to keep.

Is this financial advice?

No. Everything in the Circle is general education for Muslim households. It is not personal financial advice and it is not a fatwa. For your own situation, speak to a qualified adviser and a scholar you trust.

Who answers the questions?

The Ummah Brief team. Questions about rulings are checked with a scholar before we answer, and we never give a personal ruling.

Is it private?

Yes. Your questions are answered without your name, and we never share your details with anyone.

I am not in debt. Is it for me?

Yes. Debt is two months of the year. The rest is zakat, a cushion, your will, a second income, children, marriage and giving.
